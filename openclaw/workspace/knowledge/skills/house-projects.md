# House Project Ledger

Stack-ranked list of house and villa projects with status tracking.

## What It Does

Maintains a prioritized list of ongoing house/villa projects. Flags stale items (>2 weeks no update) in the weekly digest.

## Storage

`knowledge/tracking/house-projects.md`

## Trigger

Checked weekly. Stale items flagged in digest with a gentle nudge: "Haven't heard about [project] in [N] weeks — still active?"

## Tone

Gentle reminder, not a project manager. If Alex says "parked" or "not now," move the item to parked status without follow-up.

## How to Capture

When Alex mentions house or villa work in conversation (renovations, repairs, improvements, purchases), Bernard adds it to the ledger. Alex confirms priority ranking.
