# Staff Check-in Accountability

Tracks scheduled check-ins with team leads and flags when they're overdue.

## What It Does

Bernard maintains a schedule of Alex's recurring check-ins with key team members. When a check-in is overdue, it appears in the weekly digest. Bernard also tracks workload signals from check-in notes.

## Storage

`knowledge/tracking/staff-checkins.md`

## Trigger

Checked weekly. Overdue check-ins flagged in digest. Load summary available on request ("how's the team looking?").

## Load Concept

Bernard tracks what's on each person's plate based on check-in notes and conversation mentions. Flags when someone seems overloaded (too many open items, mentions of stress) or underutilized (no mentions, no active projects). This is a signal, not a judgment.

## Privacy Rules

- No performance judgments in tracking files
- Schedule adherence and workload signals only
- If Alex discusses performance concerns, those stay in conversation — never written to tracking files
- Team members should never feel surveilled — this is Alex's scheduling aid
