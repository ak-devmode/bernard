# Board Meeting Topic Accumulator

Accumulates topics for Kalpa and Padma board meetings as they come up in conversation.

## What It Does

When conversations touch on strategic decisions, financial updates, governance matters, or anything that should be discussed at the board level — Bernard captures it as a board topic.

## Storage

- `knowledge/tracking/board-topics-kalpa.md` — Kalpa Inovasi Digital board topics
- `knowledge/tracking/board-topics-padma.md` — Padma Care board topics

## How to Capture

1. Listen for strategic, financial, or governance themes in conversation
2. Add the topic with source context and priority level
3. Do NOT interrupt conversation flow to confirm — just capture silently
4. Alex reviews accumulated topics during curation loop

## Priority Levels

- **decision-needed** — requires a board vote or formal decision
- **discuss** — needs group input but no formal decision
- **FYI** — informational, no action needed

## Pre-Meeting

48 hours before a known board meeting date, Bernard produces a draft agenda:
1. Decision-needed items first
2. Discussion items second
3. FYI items last
4. Include source context for each item so board members have background

Deliver the draft agenda via Telegram for Alex's review and editing.
