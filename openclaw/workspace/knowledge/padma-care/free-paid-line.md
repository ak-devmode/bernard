# Padma Care — Free/Paid Line

Bernard loads this file for every community response draft.

---

## The Competitive Moat

Most healthcare providers either don't participate in community forums or pitch immediately. Consistent, knowledgeable presence with clear scope builds compounding reputation. Every helpful free answer is a deposit in the trust bank. The paid line exists because the work has real value — not because generosity has limits.

---

## Free (Community Responses)

What Bernard drafts and Alex/team posts in public forums:

- General healthcare guidance for the Bali context
- Triage advice: what questions to ask, what symptoms to watch for
- BPJS navigation basics (enrollment, referral flow, coverage categories)
- Hospital and clinic recommendations (general — not personalized care plans)
- Insurance coverage general guidance (what typical policies cover/don't in Indonesia)
- "What to expect" framing for common procedures or visits

**The rule:** If the answer helps everyone reading the thread, it's free.

---

## Paid — Healthcare Advocacy

When the situation requires someone in the room, on the phone, or navigating bureaucracy on the patient's behalf:

- In-hospital navigation and accompaniment (translating, advocating with doctors)
- BPJS complexity handling (claim disputes, multi-hospital referrals, upgrade navigation)
- Dealing with Indonesian medical bureaucracy (letters, approvals, inter-facility transfers)
- Diagnosis explanation and second opinion coordination
- Emergency coordination (ambulance, hospital selection, family communication)

**The signal:** The person can't solve this from a forum thread. They need someone there.

---

## Paid — Healthcare Concierge

Ongoing relationship for people who want healthcare handled proactively:

- Ongoing GP/specialist coordination
- Health check planning and scheduling
- Preventive care scheduling (vaccinations, screenings, dental)
- Medical records management (collecting, translating, organizing across providers)
- Peace of mind retainer — someone to call when something comes up

**The signal:** The person wants a system, not a one-off answer.

---

## The Transition

When a thread crosses from free to paid territory, use this framing:

> "That's exactly the kind of thing we help with professionally — [specific thing they need]. Here's how to reach us: [contact]"

**Rules for the transition:**
- Only after providing genuine value in the thread first
- Never in emergency threads — help first, always
- One transition per thread maximum — never double-pitch
- Match specificity: name the exact service they need, don't link a generic page
- Tone: confident and clear, not apologetic ("we can help" not "if you're interested maybe...")
