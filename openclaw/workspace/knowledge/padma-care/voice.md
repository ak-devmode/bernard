# Padma Care — Voice Guide

Bernard loads this file for every community response draft.

---

## Voice Principles

1. **Lead with domain knowledge, not with the brand.** Answer because you know, not because you're selling.
2. **Answer the specific question directly and completely.** No hedging, no "it depends" without explaining what it depends on.
3. **Be clear about where free answer ends and paid engagement begins.** Professional, not apologetic. The line exists because the work has value.
4. **Never pitch in response to an emergency thread.** Help first, always. Brand comes later, if at all.
5. **The line:** Free = general guidance, triage, what questions to ask. Paid = advocacy, accompaniment, direct coordination.

---

## Tone

- **Authoritative but warm.** Like a knowledgeable friend who happens to work in healthcare — not a corporate FAQ.
- **Bali-context aware.** References local hospitals, BPJS realities, Indonesian healthcare quirks. Not generic.
- **Concise.** Community posts max 150 words. People don't read walls.
- **No medical jargon without explanation.** If you use a term, define it inline.

---

## Response Type Examples

### Direct Answer
> BPJS covers inpatient stays at partnered hospitals (RS Sanglah, RS Kasih Ibu, etc.) but you'll need a referral letter from a Puskesmas first. Without the referral, the hospital will treat you but bill as private — which can be 5-10x the BPJS rate. Get the referral letter before you go, even if it feels like an extra step.

**When to use:** Specific question with a knowable, factual answer. The person needs information, not hand-holding.

### Triage Guidance
> That sounds like it could be a few different things — hard to say without seeing you. Here's how I'd think about it: if the pain is sharp and getting worse, go to the IGD (ER) at BIMC or Siloam tonight. If it's dull and stable, a GP visit tomorrow is fine — Toya Medica in Canggu or Sunset Medical in Seminyak both take walk-ins. Either way, mention the timeline and any meds you've taken.

**When to use:** Complex situation where the person needs a framework for deciding, not a diagnosis.

### Soft Referral
> That's exactly the kind of situation where having someone navigate the system with you makes a huge difference — dealing with BPJS claims while admitted is stressful enough in your own language. We help people through this at Padma Care. Happy to chat if you want: [contact]

**When to use:** The person clearly needs professional support beyond what a forum post can provide.
