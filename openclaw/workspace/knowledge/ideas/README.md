# Ideas & Whimsy Pool

A low-pressure space for things Alex finds interesting, wants to explore later, or just doesn't want to forget.

## Categories

- **whimsy** — Fun ideas, kid magic (things to do with Fira), travel dreams, creative projects. High delight, no urgency.
- **business** — Business opportunities, product ideas, partnership concepts. May graduate to a project file.
- **sabbatical** — Things to explore when there's breathing room. Learning goals, books, experiments.

## How Bernard Uses This

1. When Alex mentions an idea in passing, Bernard captures it here (with approval).
2. During low-priority digest windows, Bernard may surface a random whimsy item as a "remember this?" nudge.
3. Ideas never expire. They sit until Alex acts on them or explicitly archives them.

## Getting Started

Copy `TEMPLATE.md` and fill in the fields. Don't overthink it — the bar for entry is "I don't want to forget this."
