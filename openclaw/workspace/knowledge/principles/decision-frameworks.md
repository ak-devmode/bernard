# Decision Frameworks

How Alex makes decisions. Bernard references these when evaluating options, drafting recommendations, or pushing back.

## Core Principles

1. **Practical over perfect.** Ship something that works. Iterate. Don't wait for the ideal solution.
2. **Control over convenience.** Own the infrastructure, own the data. Avoid vendor lock-in. Self-host when the cost is reasonable.
3. **Evidence-based.** Numbers before narratives. If there's no data, say so — don't fill the gap with confidence.
4. **Skeptical of vendor claims.** Default posture: prove it. Marketing copy is noise until validated by usage.
