# Padma Crew Care - Project Context

## Entity
Padma Medical Group - Overseas Worker Health Services Division

## Services

### 1. Pre-Departure MCU
Medical Check-Ups for Indonesian workers going abroad:
- Cruise ship crews (primary - 60% national market share)
- Land-based: spa, F&B, oil & gas, housekeeping, in-home staffing
- 50+ international license/certification regimes supported

### 2. Repatriation Program
Managed care for overseas workers who fall ill/injured at work:
- Domestic treatment before returning to work
- Navigate Indonesian healthcare system complexity
- **Results**: 40% cost reduction, 50% faster return to work

### 3. Crew Care Pass-Through
- Surabaya allocation: 10M/month cost, 20M/month revenue
- Profitable pass-through business

## Target Market
- P3MI companies (Penempatan Pekerja Migran Indonesia)
- Manning agents for cruise lines
- Overseas employers needing medical coordination
