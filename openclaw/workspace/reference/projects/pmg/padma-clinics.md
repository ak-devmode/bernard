- 17+ years operating history
- Core business: Medical Check-Ups (MCU) for overseas workers

## Core Business: Seafarer MCU
- **Market share**: 60% national (cruise crew), 40% Surabaya after 4 years
- **Target**: Cruise ship crew medical certifications
- **Workflow**: Manning agents → Padma → Cruise companies
- **Agent fees**: 5-25% of revenue (typically 10%)
- **Sole appointments**: 2 clients only (requires VP+ consensus at cruise cos)

## Authorizations
- KTKLN designation (Bali, Surabaya) - BP2MI sarkes ecosystem
- 50+ international license/certification regimes
- Full Indonesian authority accreditation
- GAMCA (Gulf states) - separate authorization track
- Taiwan CDC - separate application needed

## Competitive Landscape
- **PHC**: Government-owned, regulatory capture, slow-walked Padma license 1yr, required ~200M bribe
- **Indosehat model**: Paper-mill clinics, checkbox service, wins industrial segment
- **Puri Medika**: Main Bali competitor, not in Surabaya

## Growth Attempts (Limited Success)
- Industrial/corporate MCU: Buyers want checkbox, Indosehat wins
- Hospitality: Relationship-dependent, GM turnover kills deals
- O&G: Supply chain fragmented, consultants want upfront fees
