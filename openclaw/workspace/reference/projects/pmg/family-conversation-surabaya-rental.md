## 1. Business Performance Summary

### 1.1 Revenue Growth Trajectory
- **2025 Total Revenue:** 4.46B IDR (+6.4% YoY)
- **2024 Total Revenue:** 4.19B IDR (+132% from 2023)
- **2023 Total Revenue:** 1.81B IDR (first full year)

**Revenue by Service Line (2025):**
- MCU (Medical Check-ups): 3.47B (78% of revenue) - **core engine**
- Crew Care: 291M (6%)
- Obat (Pharmacy): 317M (7%)
- Poly (Outpatient): 247M (6%)
- Vaksin (Vaccinations): 132M (3%)

### 1.2 MCU Business Momentum
MCU is our primary growth driver and continues accelerating:
- 2023: 1.48B
- 2024: 2.41B (+63%)
- 2025: 3.47B (+44%)

This is healthy, sustainable growth in our core competency serving the cruise ship seafarer market where we hold ~40% market share in Surabaya.

### 1.3 Margin Improvement
**Gross Margin:** 62% in 2025 (up from 48% in 2024)

The 2024 margin was artificially depressed by two factors:
1. **Obat revenue anomaly:** 1B in pharmacy revenue from two high-cost Crew Care cases (one cancer patient receiving 75M/month treatments for 7-8 months until passing away, plus offshore workers with expensive long-term prescriptions)

2025 represents normalized operations. We expect 2026 margins to track closer to 2025 levels.

### 1.4 Cost Structure
**Total Operating Expenses:** 3.09B IDR
- Salaries: 2.22B (72% of opex)
- Operational costs: 864M (28% of opex)

**Key Point:** Operating expenses are largely fixed. The facility requires minimum staffing levels to maintain our klinik utama license (in-house lab, 5-6 specialists on staff) and operate as a medical facility. We cannot meaningfully reduce costs without closing the clinic.

### 1.5 2025 Bottom Line
**Operating Loss:** 310M IDR (revenue 4.46B - COGS 1.68B - opex 3.09B)

**Net Loss (accounting):** 1.16B IDR (includes intercompany allocations and support costs)

**Key Point:** This is a big hill to climb, but the trajectory is clear. MCU growth continues accelerating, margins have normalized, and operating losses are narrowing. With current momentum, we can get there in 2026.


### 1.6 Q4 2025 Performance Zoom In
**Performance roughly matched Q3** - Operating loss in the 250 jt range - accounting loss in the 500 jt range

We did not see the development we hoped for despite a strong December, because of a very poor November result.

## 2. Investment to Date

### 2.1 Total Capital Deployed
**7.7 Billion IDR** net investment in the Surabaya clinic since inception (2022-2025)

This includes:
- Initial facility modifications and medical build-out
- Equipment and systems installation
- License acquisition and compliance costs
- Working capital
- Operating losses funded during ramp-up phase (2022-2025)

**Important note:** Our high water mark was north of 8 billion IDR. Cash flow from operations during our cash flow positive months has paid down the cumulative investment to the current 7.7B level, demonstrating the business is generating real cash in strong months.

### 2.2 Good Faith Payments
**200M IDR paid to family** during partnership period:
- 100M in Year 1
- 100M in Year 2

These payments were made as good faith gestures during the profit-sharing arrangement, despite the clinic not reaching profitability. This establishes our baseline expectation for property value at **100M IDR per year**.

### 2.3 Property Improvements (2025)
**237M IDR spent on building maintenance and improvements** in 2025 alone.

This was done without discussion or deduction from any partnership arrangements. These investments maintain the building to medical facility standards and preserve asset value for the property owner.

**Important Context:** Under a standard commercial lease, major maintenance costs of this magnitude would typically be:
- Shared between landlord and tenant, OR
- Fully covered by the landlord, OR
- Deducted from rent when tenant performs the work

We absorbed this cost entirely in 2025 under the existing partnership structure.
