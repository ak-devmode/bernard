MCU is our primary growth driver and continues accelerating:

The 2024 margin was artificially depressed by two factors:
1. **Obat revenue anomaly:** 1B in pharmacy revenue from two high-cost Crew Care cases (one cancer patient receiving 75M/month treatments for 7-8 months until passing away, plus offshore workers with expensive long-term prescriptions)

**Total Operating Expenses:** 3.09B IDR
- Salaries: 2.22B (72% of opex)
- Operational costs: 864M (28% of opex)

**Key Point:** Operating expenses are largely fixed. The facility requires minimum staffing levels to maintain our klinik utama license (in-house lab, 5-6 specialists on staff) and operate as a medical facility. We cannot meaningfully reduce costs without closing the clinic.

### 1.5 2025 Bottom Line
**Operating Loss:** 310M IDR (revenue 4.46B - COGS 1.68B - opex 3.09B)

**Net Loss (accounting):** 1.16B IDR (includes intercompany allocations and support costs)

**Key Point:** This is a big hill to climb, but the trajectory is clear. MCU growth continues accelerating, margins have normalized, and operating losses are narrowing. With current momentum, we can get there in 2026.

## 3. Looking Ahead - 2026 Outlook

### 3.1 Path to Breakeven
**Confidence Level:** 80%

Based on current MCU growth trajectory, we project **operational breakeven (cash flow positive) in 2026**.

**What breakeven means:**
- Revenue covers cost of goods sold
- Revenue covers operating expenses (salaries + operational costs)
- Revenue covers rent at market rates
- Does NOT mean recovery of the 7.7B invested to date

**Key assumptions:**
- MCU revenue continues growing 20-30% (conservative relative to 44% in 2025)
- Gross margins remain stable at ~60%
- Operating expenses remain flat (already at minimum viable staffing)

### 3.2 What Gets Us There
We need approximately **1B IDR additional MCU revenue** to reach breakeven.

At current growth rates:
- 2025 MCU: 3.47B
- 2026 Target: 4.5B+ MCU
- Gap: ~1B (achievable with 29% growth, below 2025's 44%)

The trend lines support this timeline with reasonable confidence.

### 3.3 Investment Recovery Timeline
Even at 2026 breakeven, **we will not recover the 7.7B investment for many years**.

The surrounding area is mixed residential-commercial with **substantial vacancy**.

**Finding an alternative tenant** for a property of this type in this location would be challenging:
- Not prime commercial real estate location
- Limited foot traffic for retail/hospitality
- Requires tenant with specific use case
- Building modifications limit potential uses

### 4.3 Our Commitment vs. Alternatives
We are committed to maintaining national coverage (Bali, Surabaya, Jakarta) for strategic reasons.

However, **we cannot operate unprofitably indefinitely**. If forced to choose between:
- Unreasonable rental terms that push breakeven beyond 2026-2027, OR
- Exploring alternative locations or business models

We would need to consider all options. The 60+ licenses tied to this location create substantial friction for relocation, but sustained additional losses are not sustainable.
