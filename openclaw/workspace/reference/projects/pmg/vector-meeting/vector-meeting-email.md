Team,

Thursday afternoon I want us to do something different. I'm calling it the Vector Meeting.

Here's the idea: Padma sits at the intersection of healthcare and technology in a way that almost no one else in Indonesia does. We run clinics. We engage patients directly. We build our own tech. That combination gives us options that most companies don't have — but only if we understand where things are going and what role we want to play.

This meeting is a brainstorm. Not a strategy session, not a planning meeting. I want us to think big — bigger than operational improvements, bigger than "how do we use AI to fill forms." I want us to think about where healthcare technology is heading globally, what that means for Indonesia, and where Padma should be pointed over the next five years.

**The frame is simple. I need us to map four things:**

1. Where is the global bleeding edge of healthcare technology right now — not in university labs, but actually deployed and working in real clinical/commercial settings?
2. Where is the equivalent capability available in Indonesia today?
3. Where will the global bleeding edge be in 5 years?
4. Where will Indonesia be in 5 years?

These four points create a shape — a space of realistic opportunity. Our job is to figure out where inside that space Padma should be heading.

**What I need from each of you before Thursday:**

Come prepared with 2–3 examples for each of the four points above. They can be from any domain — diagnostics, imaging, patient engagement, care coordination, data/analytics, robotics, insurance, whatever catches your attention. Don't filter yourself. If something seems too ambitious or too far out, bring it anyway. That's the point.

Where to look: industry news, competitor moves, things you've seen at conferences or in conversations, technology you've read about. Google is fine. Talking to people you know in the industry is better. The goal is for each of us to bring real data points — not opinions — so we have an informed discussion.

**What this is NOT:**

This is not about our current projects or operational priorities. We'll get to how this connects to what we're building — but Thursday is about lifting our heads up and looking at the horizon. I want creative thinking, not task management.

I attach short vision document  to give more context. But the real content comes from what you all bring to the table.

See you Thursday.

Alex
