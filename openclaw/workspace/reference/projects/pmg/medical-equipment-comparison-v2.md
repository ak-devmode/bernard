## 3. DR vs CR RATIONALE (1200/month Volume)

**Why DR over CR at your volume:**

- **Consumables elimination:** CR requires cassettes + imaging plates (IPs) with ongoing replacement costs. DR has zero consumables.
  - CR annual consumables: ~70M IDR (IP replacement + cassette wear)
  - DR annual consumables: 0 IDR

- **Workflow efficiency:** DR processing time 5-10 seconds vs CR 60-90 seconds per image.
  - Time saved: 1.5 min/X-ray × 14,400/year = 360 hours/year
  - Labor value: ~16M IDR/year
  - Faster throughput = more patients possible

- **5-year TCO comparison:**
  - CR: 440M upfront + 355M consumables/service = 795M total
  - DR: 700M upfront + 200M service = 900M total
  - DR breaks even at 3-4 years, then saves 15-20M/year

- **Technology obsolescence:** CR is declining technology, parts availability decreasing, hospitals upgrading to DR (glutting used CR market).

- **Image quality:** DR better dynamic range, fewer retakes, no cassette artifacts.

## 4. USED EQUIPMENT MARKET ANALYSIS

### Indonesia Used Market Dynamics

**Market characteristics:**
- Limited domestic supply (fewer hospital upgrades vs Western markets)
- Import barriers (10-15% duties + 50-100M shipping + customs delays)
- Parts scarcity (after-market support challenging)
- Growing demand (clinics upgrading from analog)
- Budget-conscious buyers

**Result:** Indonesian used equipment trades at HIGHER percentage of new price than Western markets.

### Key Takeaways

**Indonesia premium over Western used prices:**
- Ultrasound: +15-25% (import costs + limited supply)
- DR: +10-20% (heavier = higher shipping costs)
- Example: DC-70 5yr USA $5-6k (75-90M), Indonesia 90-110M

**Distressed discount (failed clinic vs hospital upgrade):**
- Failed/closing clinic: 25-40% below normal used price
- Must-sell urgency: Cash payment + immediate removal = leverage
- Example: Normal used FDR Go 500M → Distressed 350M (30% discount)

**Critical factors affecting distressed pricing:**
- Probe/detector condition (most expensive component)
- Service history documentation (well-maintained = premium)
- Reason for sale (bankruptcy = deep discount)
- Seller urgency (same-day removal = leverage)
- Geography (in-country = avoid import costs)

**Procurement strategy for used:**
- Target: Distressed clinic sales (not hospital upgrades)
- Opening offer: 40-50% of normal used market value
- Cash + immediate removal: Additional 10-20% leverage
- Due diligence: Power on test, probe/detector inspection, service logs, DICOM test
- Service verification: Confirm parts available + provider supports brand
- Red flags: Can't demonstrate function, no probes/detectors, locked software, >8 years old

**Age cutoff rationale (≤6 years / 2019+):**
- DICOM compatibility: 2019+ equipment has mature DICOM 3.0 implementation
- Detector life: 6yr old detector has 60-70% life remaining at 1200/month volume
- Parts availability: Manufacturers support 10+ years (2019 = supported until 2029+)
- Technology gap: 2019 vs 2025 evolutionary (not revolutionary) except AI processing
- Service viability: Providers still trained on 2019 models

**FHIR integration note:**
- 2024+ premium equipment may output FHIR ImagingStudy resources natively
- Budget tier (Mindray 280, older GE/Fujifilm) = DICOM only
- FHIR capability saves ~10-15 hours middleware development
- Not worth 50-100M equipment premium (you're building middleware anyway for SATU SEHAT)
- DICOM Worklist (MWL) universally supported = adequate for integration

## 5. TECHNOLOGY INFLECTION POINTS (2018-2025)

### Major Transitions Affecting Equipment Selection

**2018-2019: Cesium Iodide (CsI) Detector Maturation**
- CsI scintillator became standard (was premium)
- 20-30% better DQE, lower dose requirements
- All manufacturers adopted
- Pre-2018 detectors noticeably noisier

**2020-2021: High Fill Factor Detectors**
- Pixel fill factor increased 70-75% → 80-85%+
- Better spatial resolution
- Minor clinical impact (mostly pediatrics/extremities)

**2021-2022: Deep Learning Image Reconstruction - MAJOR LEAP**
- Neural network-based image processing vs algorithmic
- 30-50% noise reduction at same dose OR same quality at 40-50% lower dose
- Available on: GE Critical Imaging Suite (2021+), Fujifilm Intelligent Processing (2022+)
- NOT available on budget tier (Mindray 280/330)
- Gap: 2024 GE with AI vs 2019 GE without AI = 40-50% image quality difference
- Can add via cloud AI post-acquisition but expensive at volume (10-30 USD/study = 150-450k IDR)

**DICOM/PACS Compatibility Issues:**
- DICOM 3.0 base standard unchanged (backward compatible)
- 2019-2020: IHE profile requirements tightened (more DICOM tags required)
- 2020+: TLS encryption often required by modern PACS (older equipment may need VPN workaround)
- 2022+: IPv6 support (not urgent, dual-stack networks handle)
- 2024+: FHIR output capability (budget tier = DICOM only)

**Integration compatibility:**
- 2019+ equipment: Works with modern PACS (may need config tweaking = 3-5 hours)
- Pre-2019: May have compatibility niggles requiring more engineering time
- WellMed FHIR-first architecture: DICOM → middleware → FHIR works fine (equipment FHIR output not critical)

### Used vs New Technology Gap

**Used 2019-2020 GE/Fujifilm vs New 2025 Mindray:**
- Image quality at acquisition (no AI): ~equivalent (both algorithmic processing)
- DICOM compatibility: Both work (2019 needs more config time)
- Service/parts: Used = higher cost + availability risk
- Detector age: 5-6 years old = 60-70% life remaining vs 100% new
- AI processing: Neither has on-device AI at budget tier
- Cloud AI: Works with both (DICOM output → cloud processing)

**Conclusion:** Technology gap not significant enough to justify used equipment risk unless price <550M with verified condition + service support.
