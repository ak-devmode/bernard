# PMG 2026 Operating Plan - Analysis & Issues

**Prepared:** Feb 2026 | **For:** Board Review | **Version:** Draft 2

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The 2026 plan projects **20% revenue growth** to 116.9B with margin expansion (EBITDA 19.5% → 22.0%). Growth driven by Padma Care scaling and recovered RCG relationship. OpEx held roughly flat despite growth, driving operating leverage.

| Metric | 2025 Est | 2026 Budget | YoY |
|--------|----------|-------------|-----|
| Net Revenue | 97.4B | 116.8B | +20% |
| Gross Margin | 39.4B (40.5%) | 46.4B (39.7%) | +18% |
| EBITDA | 19.0B (19.5%) | 25.7B (22.0%) | +35% |
| Net Income | 15.0B (15.4%) | 22.0B (18.8%) | +46% |

## 5. Key Themes for Board

**1. RCG is the big swing factor**
- New objective adds 8B rev / 1.9B profit

**2. Operating leverage story is clean**
- OpEx flat (+1.2%) while revenue +20%
- EBITDA margin expansion driven by scale, not cuts

**3. Concentration risk unchanged**
- 60% of revenue from Crew Care
- Mitigation: Padma Care diversification (but still <1% of rev)

**4. Surabaya still small**
- 5.7B (5% of total) - is this the ePMI growth or something else?
- 1000 ePMI target = 400M incremental
- the rest is from natural share accumulation in MCU pelaut (we can't wait)
