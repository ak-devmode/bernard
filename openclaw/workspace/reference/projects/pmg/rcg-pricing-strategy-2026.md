## 2. Pricing Change Analysis

### Current Pricing Structure
- Onboarding: $300/case
- Monthly management: $150/case
- Standard visit: $75/visit
- Legal conversion fee: $200/case (when case converts to legal)

### Proposed Pricing Structure
- Onboarding: $300/case (unchanged)
- Monthly management: $180/case (+$30, +20%)
- Standard visit: $85/visit (+$10, +13%)
- Virtual visit: $45/visit (new option)
- Legal conversion fee: $0 (eliminated, was $200)

### Financial Impact Calculation

**Based on 2024 volumes:**
- Legal cases: 58 
- Total cases: 263
- Estimated visits: ~5,000

| Virtual visit savings | 450 eligible × -$40 | -$18,000 |

## 3. Virtual Visit Opportunity Analysis

### Methodology
Analyzed service tickets across all customers to identify "virtual consult eligible" candidates:
- Repeat physical therapy sessions
- Follow-up lab reviews
- Routine check-ins (low complexity)
- Excluded: Initial evaluations, post-op visits, imaging coordination

### Findings - All Customers
- Total virtual-eligible tickets: ~2,200
- Service :: Visit tickets: Mixture of service and treatment categories

### Findings - RCG Specific
| Category | Count | Virtual Eligible | Notes |
|----------|-------|------------------|-------|
| Legal cases | ~1,400 | **0** | Require in-person accompaniment (security, isolation protocols) |
| Normal repatriation | ~450 | **450** | Eligible for virtual follow-ups |
| **Total** | **1,850** | **450** | **24% actually eligible** |

**Key Insight:** The majority of RCG's virtual-eligible visits are actually legal cases which cannot transition to virtual due to enhanced protocols (security monitoring, isolated patient management, prevention of attorney/scout contact).

**Real savings potential:**
- 450 eligible visits × $40 savings ($85 - $45) = **$18,000/year**
- Conservative estimate for proposal: **$10-15k** (accounts for adoption curve)

## 4. Strategic Rationale

### Why This Pricing Change?

**1. Legal Case Management Investment**
Current enhanced protocols for legal cases (implemented during/post investigation):
- Senior physician oversight and review
- Isolated patient management (separate hotels, no patient comingling)
- Security monitoring to prevent scout/attorney contact
- Enhanced documentation for arbitration/court defense
- Legal-ready medical records and timeline preparation

**Cost reality:** These protocols are significantly more expensive than standard case management, yet the $200 legal fee barely covered incremental costs.

**2. Alignment of Incentives**
Removing the $200 legal conversion fee eliminates any perception that Padma benefits from legal conversions. Both parties are now fully aligned on minimizing legal cases.

**3. Operational Sophistication**
Virtual visits demonstrate:
- Service maturity and innovation
- Operational efficiency improvements
- Platform capabilities beyond basic coordination
- Differentiation from competitors
