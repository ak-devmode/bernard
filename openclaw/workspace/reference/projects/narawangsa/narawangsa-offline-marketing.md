# Narawangsa Offline Marketing (Agent) - Project Context (Private)

## Property
Narawangsa Villas - Twin 4BR luxury villas in Umalas/Canggu, Bali

## Goal
Move away from OTA dependency to agent/DMC direct relationships
- Better brand/pricepoint/amenity alignment
- Higher booking values ($3,000+ target)
- Quality guest filtering

## Commission Structure
- Travel advisors: 10% commission
- DMCs: 20-25% net rate discount

## Rate Card Structure (DMC)

### Published Rates (Client-facing)
- Fully loaded with amenities (breakfast, airport transfer, etc.)
- Seasonal bands: Low / Shoulder / High / Peak
- Include minimum stay requirements

### Net Rates (DMC pays)
- 20-25% below published
- Base accommodation rate
- Seasonal variations, simpler structure
