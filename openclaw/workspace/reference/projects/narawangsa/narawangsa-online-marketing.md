## Positioning Strategy

### Do Say
- "Hidden gem" / insider find
- Sophisticated privacy
- Quality over volume
- Premium (not "luxury")
- Location arbitrage: 30min to Seminyak/Canggu/Ubud, avoids traffic

### Don't Say
- "Luxury" (overused, triggers wrong expectations)
- "Hidden gem" (overused)
- Heavy cultural storytelling in awareness stage
- Instagram-style superlatives
- Individual villa names (use "Narawangsa" throughout)

## OTA Presence
- Airbnb, Booking.com active
- Consistent rates across platforms
- 90-day forward pricing includes premium services
- OTA bookings: breakfast/transfers are upsells
- Goal: Reduce OTA dependency

## Platform-Specific Constraints
| Platform | Limits |
|----------|--------|
| Booking.com | 150 char headline, 2000 char description |
| Airbnb | 500 char summary, 1000 char space description |

## Content by Funnel Stage
| Stage | Approach |
|-------|----------|
| Awareness | Visual appeal, location benefits, key amenities |
| Consideration | Unique story, differentiation, insider positioning |
| Booking | Practical concerns, reassurance, clear value |
| Post-Stay | Cultural story payoff, repeat visit encouragement |
