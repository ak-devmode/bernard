# Padma Care Marketing & Sales Template

## Core Context (Include in Every Request)

**Service Overview:**
- Medical concierge/advocacy service for expat families in Bali
- Monthly membership (350,000 IDR/~$24 USD) + a la carte options
- On-call agent + case management doctors for care coordination
- Negotiated pricing at 7/11 preferred hospitals (local rates regardless of immigration status)
- Curated network of 108 vetted doctors from 4000+ available
- 40-minute free consultation for prospects

**Target Profile (Current Reality):**
- Primary: Expat families with international insurance (2 foreigners or 1 foreign/1 local)
- Must have existing insurance (gateway condition) - Padma Care supplements, doesn't replace
- **NEW PRIMARY SEGMENT:** Relocating expat families (pre-arrival planning mode)
- Secondary: Recently arrived families (within 2-3 weeks of bad healthcare experience)
- Proven need for healthcare navigation/advocacy in Bali
- Exclude: Digital nomads (low conversion), severely ill patients (prefer individual companions)
- Decision window: 2-3 weeks post-negative healthcare experience before emotional urgency fades (for reactive segment)
- Zero successful referrals from traditional referral program

**Core Pain Points (Priority Order):**
1. **Pricing/Overbilling** - Being "taken advantage of" by hospitals, unclear/inflated pricing
2. **Decision Anxiety** - Not knowing where to go, which doctor to see, what's "best"
3. **Quality Concerns** - Misdiagnosis, overtreatment, unqualified referrals, poor communication
4. **System Navigation** - Bureaucracy, long waits, language barriers, billing issues

**Competitive Position:**
- "Healthcare advocate" NOT "medical concierge" (too ritzy/premium perception failed)
- Flat monthly rate vs. competitors' hourly billing (major differentiator)
- Negotiated pricing + system navigation vs. individual practitioners with medical expertise only
- Preventive guidance vs. crisis-only intervention
- Supplement existing insurance vs. replacement coverage

## Voice & Positioning Evolution

### **Core Voice Shift: Crisis → Planning**

**OLD (Crisis/Reactive):**
- "When your child gets sick..."
- "After a bad hospital experience..."
- "You'll get overcharged..."
- "We're here when crisis strikes"
- Tone: Fear-based, protective, rescue service

**NEW (Proactive/Planning):**
- "Before your child gets sick..."
- "To avoid the bad hospital experience everyone else had..."
- "Here's how pricing actually works—and how to pay local rates"
- "Healthcare setup is part of smart relocation planning"
- Tone: Competent guide, insider knowledge, empowering

### **Key Messaging Reframes**

| Avoid (Crisis) | Use Instead (Proactive) |
|----------------|-------------------------|
| "Medical concierge" | "Healthcare advocate" / "Your Personal Health Advisor" |
| "When you need us" | "From day one, here's how it works" |
| "Insurance for emergencies" | "Essential infrastructure for life in Bali" |
| "We rescue families" | "We help smart families set up properly" |
| "Premium service" | "Part of your Bali checklist" |
| "Crisis intervention" | "Preventive setup + ongoing support" |

### **The Core Reframe**
**NOT:** "Insurance for when something goes wrong"  
**BUT:** "The healthcare setup every prepared family has"

**The psychological shift:** It's not about *if* you'll need healthcare in Bali. It's about *how* you'll handle healthcare in Bali. Every family needs doctors eventually—the question is whether you figure it out stressed or set it up while thinking clearly.

## Pre-Arrival Lead Nurture Sequence (10-8-4 Week Model)

### **Week 10: "Setup Checklist" Touch**
**Goal:** Position healthcare as natural part of relocation planning  
**Tone:** Helpful peer, project manager energy  
**Emotional driver:** Checklist completion satisfaction

**Key Message Points:**
- "One thing most families forget until it's too late"
- Healthcare setup belongs alongside school, housing, visa
- "You can pick your start date—most families choose coverage to begin the day they land"
- Social proof: "The families who set this up beforehand tell us it's the smartest $24/month they spend"
- **Friction removal:** "Takes 10 minutes, then it's checked off"

**CTA Options:**
1. Primary: "Get this sorted" → Direct signup link
2. Secondary: "Want to discuss your family's situation?" → 40-min consultation

**Message Opening Examples:**
- "Saw you're planning your Bali move for [DATE]—smart timing! Quick question: have you sorted your healthcare setup yet?"
- "One thing I notice most relocating families forget: healthcare navigation. Here's why the smart ones handle it early..."
- "You're probably deep in visa paperwork and school research. This is the one setup task that takes 10 minutes and saves endless stress later..."

### **Week 8: "Insider Knowledge" Touch**
**Goal:** Educate on system realities they don't know yet  
**Tone:** Conspiratorial insider, "here's what they won't tell you"  
**Emotional driver:** FOMO (others know something I don't)

**Key Message Points:**
1. **The Pricing Reality:** "Same X-ray, different price based on your passport. We've negotiated local rates regardless of visa status. Most families recoup the annual membership on their first ER visit."

2. **The Decision Paralysis Problem:** "Bali has 4,600+ doctors. When your kid needs care at 7pm on a Sunday, you won't have time to research. We've already vetted 108 specialists you can trust."

3. **The Relationship Advantage:** "Facebook groups give you 12 conflicting opinions. Your PHA gives you one expert recommendation—same person every time, builds a relationship with your family."

**Pattern Interrupt:** "This isn't 'just in case' coverage. This is 'how healthcare actually works here' knowledge you need from day one."

**CTA:** "Want to talk through how this works for your family? Free 40-minute consultation—no pressure, just answers."

**Message Opening Examples:**
- "3 things about Bali healthcare that surprise every family we work with..."
- "Quick education on how medical pricing actually works here (hint: your passport matters)..."
- "The Our Year in Bali community asked us to share this with relocating families..."

### **Week 4: "Decision Window Closing" Touch**
**Goal:** Create healthy urgency around approaching move date  
**Tone:** Genuine check-in, helpful project manager  
**Emotional driver:** Pre-move chaos avoidance

**Key Message Points:**
- "[DATE] is coming up fast—have you sorted healthcare yet?"
- Social proof: "Families who join before arrival say the same thing: 'Best decision—knowing exactly where to go when [child] got sick in week 2 was priceless'"
- **Remove friction:** "Coverage starts the day you land. Your PHA reaches out within 24 hours. 10 minutes of forms, then done."
- **Reality check:** "The last few weeks before moving are chaos—this is one thing you don't want to be researching while unpacking boxes."

**CTA Dual-Track:**
1. "Ready to get this sorted?" → Direct signup
2. "Still have questions?" → Consultation booking

**Message Opening Examples:**
- "Quick question about your [DATE] arrival—still have healthcare on your 'figure out later' list?"
- "You've checked off school, housing, visa. Healthcare is the last big piece most families handle before arrival."
- "One month out! Here's what families tell us they wish they'd done before landing..."

### **Post-Arrival Hail Mary (If No Pre-Arrival Conversion)**
**Timing:** 3 days after arrival date  
**Tone:** Welcoming, no guilt, door remains open

**Message:** "Hey [Name]—welcome to Bali! Hope the first few days have been smooth. Quick note: most families who join tell us they wish they'd done it before arrival. If you're already dealing with visa runs, school admin, and settling in, healthcare navigation is probably the last thing you want to research. Happy to answer questions if it's still on your list—or we're here when you need us."

## Post-Crisis Lead Response (Already in Bali)

### **72-Hour Window (Highest Urgency)**
**Tone:** Validation + immediate solution  
**Message Focus:** "This exact thing is why we exist. Here's how we prevent it from happening again."

**Key Points:**
- Validate their experience (don't minimize)
- Name the specific failure they experienced (pricing, misdiagnosis, decision paralysis)
- Show understanding of emotional impact
- Offer immediate value: "Let's make sure this never happens to your family again"

**CTA:** Immediate consultation or direct signup with "start coverage today"

### **Week 1-2 (Strong Urgency)**
**Tone:** Peer solidarity, practical problem-solving  
**Message Focus:** Convert anger/frustration into preventive action

**Key Points:**
- "Most of our members found us exactly this way—after a confusing/expensive/scary hospital experience"
- Specific solution to their specific pain point
- "The families who join within 2 weeks tell us it's because the experience is still fresh and they want to make sure it doesn't happen again"

### **Week 3+ (Urgency Fading)**
**Approach:** Shift to proactive/educational tone (urgency window closing)  
**Reality:** Emotional energy is fading, harder to convert  
**Strategy:** Move to longer educational nurture or tag for seasonal triggers (rainy season, school year start)

## CRM Setup Recommendations

### **Lead Pipeline Stages**
1. **New Inquiry** (WhatsApp/inbound contact)
2. **Consultation Scheduled**
3. **Consultation Completed**
4. **Follow-up Nurture** (in decision window)
5. **Closed Won** / **Closed Lost**
6. **Closed Lost - Timing** (love service, not ready yet)

### **Critical Custom Properties**

**Qualifying Fields:**
- Has International Insurance? (Y/N - gateway qualifier)
- Household Composition (text: helps qualify fit)
- Current Immigration Status (dropdown)
- Lead Source Detail (Our Year in Bali, organic, word of mouth, etc.)

**Timing & Urgency Fields:**
- Prospective Move/Start Date (DATE - drives all automation)
- Current Location (Still Abroad / Already in Bali / Just Arrived)
- Days Until Move (calculated)
- Incident Date (for post-crisis leads)
- Days Since Incident (calculated - tracks urgency decay)

**Pain Point & Psychology Fields:**
- Primary Pain Point (dropdown: pricing, decision anxiety, quality, navigation)
- Triggering Event (dropdown: bad experience, planning move, friend referral, preventive)
- Relocation Stage (dropdown: researching, committed/planning, arrived <30 days, established)

**Engagement Tracking:**
- Last Meaningful Contact Date
- Consultation Completed? (Y/N)
- Nurture Sequence Stage (Week 10 / Week 8 / Week 4 / Post-arrival)

### **Lead Scoring Framework**

**Qualification Signals (+points):**
- +20: Has international insurance (qualified)
- +15: Expat family with kids
- +10: Move date 4-10 weeks out (optimal window)
- +10: From Our Year in Bali referral (pre-validated audience)
- +20: Recent negative healthcare experience (<3 weeks)
- +5: Engaged with educational content
- +5: Booked consultation

**Disqualification Signals (-points):**
- -20: Digital nomad indicators
- -10: Move date >12 weeks out (too early)
- -15: No international insurance mentioned
- -10: Already has established healthcare setup

### **Automated Task Triggers**

**Pre-Arrival Sequence:**
- 10 weeks before move → Create task: Send Week 10 touch
- 8 weeks before move → Create task: Send Week 8 touch
- 4 weeks before move → Create task: Send Week 4 touch
- 3 days after move → Create task: Send post-arrival check-in

**Post-Crisis Sequence:**
- Day 1 after incident → Create task: Immediate response
- Day 7 after incident → Create task: Follow-up check
- Day 21 after incident → Create task: Final touch or move to long nurture

**Stall Detection:**
- Deal in "Consultation Scheduled" >7 days → Task: Re-engage or reschedule
- Deal in "Follow-up" >14 days → Task: Final touch or move to Closed Lost

### **Segment-Specific Workflows**

**Pre-Arrival Relocating Families:**

- Success metric: Conversion before 4 weeks prior to move

**Post-Crisis Locals:**
- Tag: "Post-Crisis"

- Success metric: Conversion within 14 days of incident

**Recently Arrived:**
- Tag: "New Arrival"
- Nurture: Simplified messaging, acknowledge chaos
- Success metric: Conversion within 30 days of arrival

**Closed Lost - Timing:**
- Tag: "Future Prospect"
- Nurture: Monthly educational content
