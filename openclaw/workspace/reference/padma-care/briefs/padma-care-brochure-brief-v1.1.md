### 2.2 Hero Section

**Headline:**

> Your Insider Healthcare Network in Bali

> Bali has 5,000 practicing doctors. We've vetted 92 of them — the best across every specialty. Padma Care connects you to the right doctor, gets you fair pricing, and gives you a team that actually knows your health history. All in English, all on WhatsApp.

Hero should include a warm, approachable team photo — the PMG clinical team in a consultation or meeting setting (similar to current top photo). The text needs room to breathe. Do not crowd this section.

### 2.3 "What You Get" — 4 Value Blocks

Four short blocks, each with an icon and 1-2 sentences. Laid out as a 2x2 grid or horizontal strip. These justify the price the reader just saw.

> 92 vetted specialists hand-picked from 5,000+ doctors in Bali. We match you to the right one for your situation — no guesswork, no trial and error.

**Block 2: Pricing That's Fair**

**Block 3: A Team That Knows You**

Design note: Block 4 is the bridge to advocacy. It should feel like "and there's more" — not the main pitch. A subtle visual cue (arrow, "see page 2") connects it forward.

## 5. Messaging Principles (for all 4 documents)

These apply across the entire collateral family. Designer and copywriter should internalize these.

### 5.1 Do

- Lead with tangible benefits (doctors, pricing, access) — let peace of mind be the aftertaste
- Use concrete numbers: 92 specialists, 5,000 total doctors, 7 hospitals, 1,900 patients, 350K/month
- Frame Padma as insider access — "we already did the homework"
- Make signup feel like checking a box, not making a commitment
- Acknowledge the system gap without blaming hospitals or doctors

### 5.2 Don't

- Use "medical concierge" without immediately explaining what that means in practice
- Fear-monger or ambulance-chase ("what if something terrible happens?!")
- Use clinical jargon (inpatient/outpatient, triage, etc.)
- Oversell — "we will save your life" → "we make sure you're in the right hands"
- Use "queue-jumping" or other terms that imply privilege over fairness

## 7. Next Documents (Scope — Do Not Start Until Brochure is Approved)

Brief direction notes for the remaining 3 deliverables. Full briefs will be produced after the concierge brochure is finalized.

### 7.1 Advocacy — Patient-Facing (1-pager)

- Audience: patient/family already in hospital, frustrated, looking for options
- Moment: handed over by a doctor, surgeon, or hospital staff member
- Emotional frame: clarity restores agency — "you don't have to figure this out alone"
- Must include: what the service covers at a glance, pricing (250K/day base), what to expect in the first 24 hours, free orientation items (bed class chart, SOP for 1 doctor consult/day)
- Lead messaging territory: "The healthcare system here wasn't built for us."
- CTA: WhatsApp number for immediate contact

### 7.2 Advocacy — Doctor-Facing (1-pager, Indonesian)

- Audience: Indonesian hospital doctors/surgeons
- Goal: earn cooperation + referral. Communicate: referral fee (2M IDR), how Padma makes their life easier, credibility markers
- Key selling points to doctors: we defer to their clinical judgment, we communicate proactively and provide written documentation, we deliver a situation brief upon engaging, we handle medical record transfers, we produce a discharge report (English) usable for insurance claims — taking admin burden off the doctor
- Include: referral fee structure, brief credential block (5M USD hospital spend, 200 inpatients managed, 5 evacuations/repatriations, 7 hospital agreements)
- Bonus structure: +1M IDR if their referred doctor also produces a referral
- Language: Indonesian

### 7.3 Day 0 Emergency Plan (1-page, WhatsApp-sendable PDF)

- Audience: new Padma Care member, Week 2 of onboarding
- Purpose: personalized emergency reference card — print it, save to desktop, keep in your safe
- Contents: nearest ER for their location, ambulance number, 4-5 do's and don'ts for emergencies, Padma Care emergency contact info, closing line: "if you're unsure, text us first — that's what we're here for"
- Format: single A4 page, clean, high-contrast, designed to be readable in a panic
- Personalization fields: member name, area of Bali, nearest recommended ER, PHA name + WhatsApp
