## 1. Document Purpose & Context

### 1.1 What This Document Is

A single-page (front only, possibly front + back) document given by Dr. Gita to Indonesian hospital doctors and surgeons during socialization. Its purpose is twofold: earn the doctor's cooperation and motivate them to refer foreign patients to Padma Care's advocacy service.

### 1.2 Who Receives It

Indonesian specialist doctors and surgeons — primarily at the 7 partner hospitals in Bali, expanding to the broader 92-doctor network and beyond. These are busy professionals who will skim, not read. The document needs to communicate its core message in under 30 seconds.

### 1.3 What It Must Do (in order of priority)

1. **Establish credibility** — Padma is a serious medical organization, not a tourism concierge
2. **Show how Padma makes the doctor's life easier** — concrete operational benefits
3. **Communicate the referral incentive** — financial motivation, clearly stated
4. **Position the relationship as collaborative** — we defer to their clinical judgment, always
5. **Make referral action simple** — one phone call or WhatsApp message

### 1.4 What It Must NOT Do

- Position Padma as overriding or second-guessing the doctor's clinical decisions
- Feel like a pharmaceutical sales rep pitch — this is peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-customer
- Criticize the hospital or suggest the hospital is failing patients
- Overload with English — this is an Indonesian document for Indonesian doctors
- Feel cheap or promotional — quality of the document telegraphs quality of the service

## 2. Design Direction

### 2.1 Format

- Single A4 page, front only (or front + back if content requires — prefer single side)
- Print-ready, high quality stock if printed
- Same Crew Care design family but slightly more formal/clinical than the patient-facing materials
- Color palette: #505CA0 (blue-purple primary), **light green secondary** (replacing light salmon — see Figma file for exact hex), #E6854B (burnt orange CTA accent). The green differentiates this document from the patient-facing 1-pager at a glance — a doctor with both documents on their desk should be able to grab the right one instantly by color.

### 2.2 Tone

Professional, peer-to-peer, respectful. This is one medical organization talking to another medical professional. Not salesy. Not overly casual. Think: a well-written referral letter, not a marketing flyer.

### 2.3 Language Note for Designer

All body copy is in Indonesian. The designer should be comfortable laying out Indonesian text — word lengths and line breaks differ from English. Have Gita or another native speaker proof the final layout for awkward breaks or formatting issues.

## 4. Messaging Principles (Doctor-Facing Specific)

### 4.1 Core Frame

The pitch to doctors is NOT "your patients are suffering and need rescue." That implies the doctor is failing. The pitch IS: "foreign patients have different expectations that create friction for everyone. We handle that friction so you can focus on clinical care."

### 4.2 The Collaborative Positioning

From the marketing plan (Section 6.2): "We don't take any profit away from the hospital or doctor — we make everyone's life easier." This must be crystal clear. Doctors may worry that Padma will:
- Interfere with treatment decisions (addressed by "we defer to your judgment")
- Take revenue from the hospital (addressed by "we add a service layer, we don't replace hospital services")
- Create additional work (addressed by the 5 operational benefits)

### 4.3 Translation Notes

- Use formal but accessible Indonesian — not overly academic/medical journal style
- "Advokasi pasien" is the correct term — don't substitute with "pendampingan" (too casual) or "konsultasi" (different meaning)
- Medical terminology should use standard Indonesian medical register — Gita to review
- Avoid English loanwords where clear Indonesian equivalents exist, but keep terms like "WhatsApp" and specific English titles as-is

## 5. Distribution Notes

### 5.1 Primary Channel — Doctor Socialization

Dr. Gita hands this document to doctors during face-to-face socialization meetings. The document supports the conversation — it's not a substitute for it.

### 5.2 Leave-Behind

Doctors should keep this on their desk or in their drawer. The referral fee and WhatsApp number need to be findable weeks later when a relevant patient appears.

### 5.3 Print Quality

This document represents Padma's professionalism to medical peers. Print on quality stock. Do not skimp — a flimsy printout undermines the credibility pitch.

### 5.4 How the Doctor Gets the Patient-Facing Document to the Patient

Many doctors won't want to share their personal number with a patient, and hospitals may not welcome outside branded materials on their premises. This needs to be discreet and doctor-controlled — not a hospital-level display.

**Primary method (now):**
The doctor keeps a small personal supply of the patient-facing 1-pagers (Document #2) in their desk drawer — not displayed, not at a nurse station. When they encounter a struggling foreign patient, they hand it over the way they'd hand a referral note: brief, professional, low-key. "This service works with many of my foreign patients — they may be able to help you." The patient-facing document has the Padma Care WhatsApp number, not the doctor's. The doctor separately WhatsApps Padma to register the referral.

This keeps it between the doctor and the patient. No hospital management needs to know or approve anything at this stage. It's a personal recommendation, same as a doctor recommending a pharmacy or a physiotherapist.

**Future method (requires hospital relationship):**
If/when Padma has formal agreements with hospital management, branded materials (stands, QR codes) can be placed in wards or common areas. This is a Phase 2 play — don't pursue it until you have enough case volume that the hospital sees Padma as complementary to their service, not competitive.

**Design implication:** The doctor-facing document (this one, green) and the patient-facing document (Document #2, salmon/warm) must be instantly distinguishable by color. A doctor reaching into a drawer should grab the right one without reading.

### 5.5 Referral Tracking

When a doctor hands the patient document to a patient, they also WhatsApp Padma Care (+6282266323030) with the patient's name/room to register the referral. This ensures the doctor gets credit (and their referral fee) even though the patient contacts Padma directly. Gita should explain this two-step process during socialization: (1) give the document to the patient, (2) message us so we know you referred them.
