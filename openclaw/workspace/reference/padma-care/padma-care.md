# Padma Care - Project Context

## Entity
Padma Care - VIP Medical Concierge & Advocacy Service (Bali)

## Service Model
Medical navigation/advocacy for expats and VIP Indonesians in Bali

### Pricing
- Monthly membership: 350,000 IDR (~$24 USD)
- A la carte options available
- 40-minute free consultation for prospects

### Included Services
- On-call agent + case management doctors
- Care coordination across providers
- Negotiated pricing at 7/11 preferred hospitals
- Local rates regardless of immigration status
- Curated network: 108 vetted doctors (from 4000+ available)

## Target Customer Profile

### Primary Segment (NEW PRIORITY)
**Relocating expat families** - pre-arrival planning mode
- 2 foreigners OR 1 foreign/1 local
- Must have existing international insurance (gateway condition)
- Padma Care supplements, doesn't replace insurance

### Secondary Segment
Recently arrived families (within 2-3 weeks of bad healthcare experience)
- Decision window: 2-3 weeks post-negative experience before urgency fades

### Exclude
- Digital nomads (low conversion)
- Severely ill patients (prefer individual companions)

## Core Pain Points (Priority Order)
1. **Pricing/Overbilling** - Being "taken advantage of", unclear/inflated pricing
2. **Decision Anxiety** - Not knowing where to go, which doctor, what's "best"
3. **Quality Concerns** - Misdiagnosis, overtreatment, unqualified referrals, poor communication

## Marketing Approach

### Funnel Stages
| Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| Awareness | Visual appeal, key pain points |
| Consideration | Differentiation, insider positioning |
| Booking | Practical concerns, reassurance |
| Post-Stay | Full value demonstration |

### Touchpoint Cadence (Relocating Segment)
- 10 weeks before move: Initial awareness
- 8 weeks: Consideration content
- 4 weeks: Booking push
- Arrival: Onboarding

### Channels
- Relocation companies (Santa Fe, Crown, Asian Tigers)
- International schools in Asia (expat coordinators)
- Corporate relocation coordinators
- Expat Facebook groups (limited success)

## Competitive Position
- Only dedicated medical advocacy service for expats in Bali
- Core competencies: case management, first-principles medical thinking, English-speaking staff
- Honest broker between patient and healthcare system

## Referral Reality
- Zero successful referrals from traditional referral program
- Word-of-mouth works but not systematized
